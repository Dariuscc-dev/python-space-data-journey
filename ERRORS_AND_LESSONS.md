# Errors and Lessons

A running log of real errors I hit, why they happened and what I learned. Only errors that reveal a real misunderstanding are logged here, not every typo.

## Format

```md
## E00X — Short title

### Context
What I was trying to do.

### Error
The exact error message.

### My incorrect assumption
What I believed that turned out to be wrong.

### Root cause
What was actually happening.

### What I did to investigate
- Steps taken before asking for any help.

### Resolution
How I fixed it.

### Lesson
The generalisable takeaway.
```
